Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Byte Sized Software ("we", "us", or "our") built On Air as a podcast player application. This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of information when you use our app.

Information We Do Not Collect

On Air is designed with privacy as a core principle. We do not operate any servers for the app, and we do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. Specifically:

  • We do not require an account or any personal identification information
  • We do not use analytics or tracking services
  • We do not display advertisements
  • We do not sell or share any data with third parties
  • We do not have access to your favorites, listening history, or playback positions

Data Storage

Your presets (favorite podcasts) and listening history are stored locally on your device in a private database. Playback positions and episode lengths, used to resume episodes and show progress, are stored in the app's local preferences. This data never leaves your device, and deleting the app removes it.

Podcast Search and Directory

To show popular podcasts and search results, On Air sends your search terms directly to Apple's iTunes Search API. These requests are made from your device to Apple and are governed by Apple's privacy policy. We do not receive or store your searches.

Podcast Feeds, Audio, and Artwork

When you open a podcast or play an episode, the app downloads the podcast's RSS feed, artwork, and audio directly from the servers chosen by that podcast's publisher or hosting provider. As with any internet request, those servers receive standard information such as your IP address and device type, and may log it under their own privacy policies. We have no control over, and are not responsible for, the practices of podcast publishers or hosting providers.

Now Playing and Lock Screen

On Air shares the current episode's title, artwork, and playback progress with iOS so that it can appear on the Lock Screen, in Control Center, and on connected accessories. This information is handled by your device's operating system and is not sent to us.

Children's Privacy

On Air does not knowingly collect any information from children under the age of 13. The app does not require account creation or any personal information to function. Podcasts are produced by third parties, and some may contain content that is not suitable for children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
